          First time closing a company?

          Doing nothing is the expensive option

          An abandoned company doesn't quietly disappear. It keeps costing money and, after three years, it starts costing you personally.

          ₹100 a day, per form, with no ceiling

          Every missed annual filing — AOC-4, MGT-7 or MGT-7A — attracts an additional fee of ₹100 per day until filed, with no upper limit.Fee Rules Two forms unfiled for two years is roughly ₹1.4 lakh in additional fee alone.

          Three years of silence disqualifies you

          A director of a company that fails to file annual returns for three consecutive financial years is disqualified for five years§164(2) — from every board, not just that one. It follows the person, not the entity.

          The Registrar can strike it off without you

          The Registrar has its own power to remove a defunct company.§248 That leaves your compliance record unresolved, gives you no control over timing, and does nothing about GST, income-tax or PF registrations, which keep generating notices.

          And liability survives anyway

          Dissolution is not a discharge. Liability of every director, officer and member continues as though the company had not been struck off,§250 and the Tribunal can restore it within twenty years§252 — backlog and all.

          The five exits

          Your route is decided by facts, not preference

          Entity type and solvency settle this. Choosing wrong is costly — a Section 8 company that files STK-2 is rejected on the affidavit, and a company with creditors cannot use that route at all.

          Route 1 — the common one

          Voluntary strike-off

          For a private limited, OPC or unlisted public company that has stopped trading and owes nothing. You file STK-2§248 and it is processed centrally by C-PACE. No tribunal, no liquidator.

          ₹20,000 + GST · 90–150 days from filing
          Route 2 — pause instead

          Dormant status

          If there's any chance you'll use the entity again, don't kill it. Dormant status§455 is applied for in MSC-1, keeps the company alive on a light compliance load, and is reversed in MSC-4.

          ₹12,000 + GST · then ₹4,000 a year · 15–30 days
          Route 3 — non-profits

          Section 8 company

          Cannot be struck off directly. The licence is surrendered and the company converted with the Regional Director's approval in INC-18,§8(4)(ii) then struck off. Exit tax on accreted income is usually the largest figure in the exercise.

          ₹1.8L+ professional · 6–12 months · Full rate card
          Route 4 — solvent, with assets

          Voluntary liquidation

          Where the company can pay everyone but still holds assets to realise or creditors to settle. Run by an insolvency professional under Section 59 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016,IBC §59 ending in a Tribunal dissolution order.

          Quoted per engagement · 12–24 months
          Route 5 — partnerships

          Closing an LLP

          An LLP that has ceased business is struck off in Form 24, with every partner's consent, affidavits, an indemnity and a CA-certified statement of accounts. Overdue Form 8 and Form 11 come first.

          ₹12,000 + GST · 60–120 days

          Route 1 eligibility

          Can you use STK-2?

          The route generally fits

          • Companies incorporated but never traded
          • Companies that stopped trading and settled everything owing
          • Founders closing an entity before starting the real venture
          • SPVs and holding companies left over from a deal that didn't close
          • Subsidiaries no longer needed after a restructuring
          • Directors protecting their DIN from disqualification§164(2)

          It is closed to you if

          • The company is registered under Section 8. The affidavit in STK-4 requires each director to declare the company is not incorporated for charitable purposes.§8(4)(ii)
          • There are bank loans, trade creditors or any undischarged liability
          • GST registration is live or GST returns are pending
          • Income-tax returns are not filed for all applicable years
          • The company is party to pending litigation
          • There is a pending inquiry, inspection or investigation
          • The company has issued debentures or has secured creditors
          • Bank accounts are still open and carrying a balance
          Not every company qualifies for the concessional government fee

          CCFS-2026 is not available to a company already served a final strike-off notice, one that has already applied for strike-off or dormant status, a company dissolved on amalgamation, or a vanishing company.GC 01/2026 If an ROC notice has already reached you, say so at the diagnostic stage — it changes both the fee and the route.

          How Route 1 runs

          From first conversation to dissolution notice

          1. Diagnostic and route opinion

            We confirm which routes you qualify for, identify what has to be cleared first, and price the engagement in writing before you commit to anything.

          2. Clean-up

            Whatever you ticked in the builder — overdue filings, GST cancellation, EPFO and ESIC intimation, the final income-tax return, bank closure. Financial statements and the annual return must generally be filed up to the end of the financial year in which the company ceased business.Rule 4

          3. Resolutions

            The board authorises the application and appoints a signatory. The special resolution, or consent of members holding 75% of paid-up share capital, is obtained and MGT-14 filed where required.

          4. Indemnity bond and affidavit

            Every director executes an indemnity bond in STK-3 and a sworn affidavit in STK-4. Both are notarised and stamped.

          5. Statement of accounts

            Made up to a date not more than thirty days before filing, certified by a practising Chartered Accountant, in STK-8.

          6. Filing on the MCA V3 portal

            STK-2 is filed with every attachment, signed with a valid DSC. It goes to C-PACE — the Centre for Processing Accelerated Corporate Exit — which handles strike-off centrally rather than your jurisdictional ROC.

          7. Public notice and objection window

            A public notice is issued in STK-6, allowing thirty days for objections. We respond to queries and resubmissions raised in this window.

          8. Dissolution

            If no valid objection stands, the dissolution notice is published in STK-7 in the Official Gazette. The company is struck off and ceases to exist.

          The law this page relies on

          Every statutory claim above carries a reference chip. This is what each points to, so you can check us rather than take our word for it.

          Section 248, Companies Act 2013
          Power to remove a company's name from the register — on the Registrar's own motion and on a company's voluntary application in STK-2.
          Section 250, Companies Act 2013
          Effect of a company notified as dissolved. Liability of every director, officer and member continues as though the company had not been struck off.
          Section 252, Companies Act 2013
          Appeal against striking off, and restoration to the register on an application made within twenty years of dissolution.
          Section 164(2), Companies Act 2013
          Disqualification of a director of a company that has not filed financial statements or annual returns for three consecutive financial years, for five years.
          Section 455, Companies Act 2013
          Dormant company. Application in MSC-1, annual return in MSC-3, restoration to active status in MSC-4.
          Rule 4, Companies (Removal of Name of Companies from the Register of Companies) Rules, 2016
          Application for removal of name. A company is generally required to file its financial statement and annual return up to the end of the financial year in which it ceased to carry on business.
          Section 8(4)(ii), Companies Act 2013, with Rules 21 and 22, Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014
          Conversion of a Section 8 company into a company of any other kind on the Regional Director's approval in INC-18, with notice to prescribed authorities under Rule 22(2) and transfer of unutilised income to the IEPF under Rules 22(9)(c) and 22(10).
          Section 59, Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016
          Voluntary liquidation of a corporate person that has not defaulted — conducted by an insolvency professional, concluded by a dissolution order of the Tribunal.
          Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014
          Normal filing fees on the authorised share capital slab, and additional fee of ₹100 per day per form on a delayed annual filing with no upper limit.
          CCFS-2026 — MCA General Circular No. 01/2026 dated 24 February 2026, extended by General Circular No. 03/2026 dated 8 July 2026
          Companies Compliance Facilitation Scheme, 2026, issued under Section 460 read with Section 403 of the Companies Act, 2013. In force 15 April 2026 to 31 August 2026. Overdue annual filings at normal fee plus 10% of the additional fee; MSC-1 at 50%; STK-2 at 25% of the normal fee. Five categories of company excluded. Reduces the additional fee, not the normal filing fee. Does not apply to LLPs.
          Income-tax Act, 2025
          In force from 1 April 2026, governing Tax Year 2026-27 onwards. Income earned up to 31 March 2026 remains governed by the Income-tax Act, 1961. A closure engagement started now usually spans both statutes.
          Tax on accreted income
          Charged at the maximum marginal rate on the fair market value of assets less liabilities where a registered institution converts, merges or dissolves. Section numbering changed with the Income-tax Act, 2025; the applicable provision is identified in the written opinion for your facts rather than cited generically here.

          Section 8 companies
          A Section 8 company cannot apply for strike-off in STK-2. The affidavit in STK-4 requires a declaration that the company is not incorporated for charitable purposes under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 or Section 25 of the Companies Act, 1956. The licence must first be surrendered and the company converted, as in Table D, or wound up by voluntary liquidation, as in Table E.
          Exit tax on accreted income
          On conversion or dissolution of a Section 8 company holding an income-tax registration, tax on accreted income is charged at the maximum marginal rate on the fair market value of assets less liabilities. On a well-funded organisation this can exceed every other cost in Table D combined, and is quantified before the special resolution is passed. It is a tax liability of the company and is not included in any fee.
          Voluntary liquidation
          The route where the entity is solvent but holds assets to realise or creditors to pay, and the only single-step exit open to a Section 8 company without first converting. Fees exclude the liquidator's remuneration, counsel's fee before the Tribunal and publication costs, all at cost.
          LLPs
          CCFS-2026 is a scheme under the Companies Act, 2013 and its concessions do not extend to LLP filings. Additional fees on overdue Form 8 and Form 11 run on a multiplier scale under the LLP Rules and are computed on the portal at filing.
          Survival of liability
          Closure is not a discharge. Under Section 250 the liability of every director, officer and member survives dissolution as though the company had not been struck off, and the company may be restored by the Tribunal under Section 252 on an application made within twenty years. Any undistributed asset at dissolution vests in the Central Government.

          Questions we get asked

          Frequently asked

          Will the price grow after I've signed?

          Only if something turns up that you didn't tell us about — a live GST registration, an unfiled year, a bank account nobody remembered. That is exactly what the ₹7,500 diagnostic is for: it inspects the company's actual position before you commit, and the fee is credited back in full when you proceed. After the diagnostic, the engagement letter carries a fixed fee.

          What exactly does the ₹20,000 include?

          The whole strike-off filing as one fee: preparing and filing STK-2, the CA-certified statement of accounts in STK-8, board and member resolutions with MGT-14, drafting the indemnity bond STK-3 and affidavit STK-4, the first written response to a C-PACE query, and carrying it through to the dissolution notice in STK-7. Add GST at 18%. Two things sit outside it: notarisation of the affidavits, charged per director because it scales with how many of you there are, and the MCA filing fee, which is a government charge passed through at cost.

          What if I stop filing and let it lapse?

          The additional fee runs at ₹100 per day per unfiled form with no ceiling, and after three consecutive years of non-filing every director is disqualified for five years across all boards. The Registrar can strike the company off on its own motion, which leaves your record unresolved and does nothing about GST, income-tax or PF registrations. And liability survives dissolution anyway.

          Can a Section 8 company be struck off in STK-2?

          No. The affidavit in STK-4 requires each director to declare the company is not incorporated for charitable purposes under Section 8. The licence must first be surrendered and the company converted with the Regional Director's approval, or the company wound up by voluntary liquidation. Both are in the full rate card.

          How long does the concessional government fee last?

          Under CCFS-2026 the filing fee on STK-2 is 25% of the normal ₹10,000, so ₹2,500, for applications filed on or before 31 August 2026. MSC-1 is at half fee and additional fees on overdue annual filings drop to 10%. Companies already served a final strike-off notice, or that have already applied for strike-off or dormant status, are excluded. Once the scheme closes the normal fees return in full, and the estimate above updates itself.

          Can I close if GST or income-tax returns are pending?

          Not until they're filed. Pending GST returns block cancellation in REG-16, and the final return in GSTR-10 follows. Income-tax returns must be filed for all applicable years. Both are add-ons in the builder, and both are usually what determines how long a closure takes.

          Should I take dormant status instead?

          If there's any real chance you'll use the entity again, yes. Dormant status keeps the company alive on a much lighter compliance footing and is reversed in MSC-4 when you want it back. Strike-off is permanent short of a Tribunal restoration, and a restored company comes back with its entire filing backlog. The diagnostic covers this on your facts.
