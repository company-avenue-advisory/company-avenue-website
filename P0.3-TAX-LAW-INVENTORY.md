## Method

Grepped `src/` for Income-tax Act section patterns (`80*`, `44A*`, `139*`,
`192/194*`, `195/197/201/206*`, `234*`, `271*`, `276*`, `115*`, `87A`,
`10(13A)`, `16(iii)`, `24(b)`, `54*`, `56(2)`, `92*`, `149`, `269ST`,

discontinued vocabulary (`Assessment Year`, `Previous Year`, `AY 20xx`),
plus Chapter VI-A and `u/s`.

Raw grep: ~700 hits / 135 files. After removing Companies Act, CGST, FEMA,
MSMED, EPF/ESI, Copyright Act, LLP Act, Trade Marks Act, and pure code
(`section80IAC` variable names, keyword arrays), **in-scope income-tax
references: ~155 hits across 41 files.**
